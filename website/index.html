What it does

A project manager and a builder, in one.

Talk it through, get a spec

A guided interview turns a rough idea into a locked, versioned spec — with the LLM judging when each answer is actually clear enough to build from.

Build with AI

An in-app implementation agent writes real code into your repo — targeted edits, not full rewrites — gated on a clean analyze pass, streaming its work so you can watch.

A tracker that thinks

A feature board with AI tools built in: scan your repo for what exists, recommend what to build next, plan the build order into versions, and dedupe.

Run & Preview

Run your app on a simulator or emulator and watch it live inside The Forge — it even boots the simulator for you — with hot reload and the build console right there.

Architect the hard parts

It spots the features too big to one-shot, breaks them into properly sized sub-tasks, and refuses to fake work it can't do — like an ML model that needs a real dataset.

Yours, and private

Bring your own key — Ollama Cloud, Claude, or OpenAI. Your keys are stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain, the app is signed & notarized, and there's a built-in security check.

Pricing

Your provider

What building costs

You pay your AI provider for the tokens a build uses — usually cents to a couple of dollars per feature. Run a cheap, fast model for most of it, and switch to a stronger one for the hard parts.

Coming later

Managed — no key

A paid option where we run the AI for you: no provider account, no keys, one bill. For when you'd rather not manage a key. Not here yet.

Do I pay Orbital AI anything?

No. The app is free — you only pay your AI provider for what you build.

How much will building cost?

Usually cents per feature. You watch spend in your provider's dashboard, and a cheap cloud model keeps it low.

Which key should I get?

Any of Ollama Cloud, Claude, or OpenAI. Ollama Cloud is the easiest cheap start; Claude or OpenAI for top quality.

Are my keys safe?

They live in your Mac's Keychain and never leave your machine. No Forge server ever sees them.
